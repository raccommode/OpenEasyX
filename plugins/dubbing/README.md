# Local Dubbing

Select one or more output languages in the plugin settings. Each language gets its own MP4. Lip sync is off by default. Enable **Lip sync** to animate visible speakers. Changing this setting updates dubs in the selected languages. English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese are available. A video already entirely in the selected language uses the original. Videos without detected speech are skipped.

## Local processing

- Bandit v2 uses the multilingual DnR v3 checkpoint to separate dialogue from music and sound effects across the entire soundtrack. The worker mixes translated voices onto this background, including across gaps between recognized lines. Original speech already in the target language is retained for its own speaker. The translated speech bus targets −18 LUFS. [Bandit v2 model and weights](https://zenodo.org/records/12701995)
- The worker checks the remaining sound in each channel for missed dialogue. YAMNet helps distinguish speech from music and singing. The worker checks likely words against the surrounding original audio and uses DPDFNet2 to recover confirmed speech before assigning speakers. [YAMNet](https://www.tensorflow.org/hub/tutorials/yamnet) · [Sherpa-ONNX speech enhancement](https://k2-fsa.github.io/sherpa/onnx/speech-enhancement/index.html)
- Sherpa-ONNX clusters turns by speaker. Detected overlaps use local two- or three-voice separation, matched back to speaker embeddings. An entire conversation may include more than three distinct speakers. [Sherpa-ONNX diarization](https://k2-fsa.github.io/sherpa/onnx/speaker-diarization/index.html) · [SepFormer](https://huggingface.co/speechbrain/sepformer-wsj02mix)
- Faster-Whisper identifies the words and language of each isolated speaker. Helsinki-NLP OPUS-MT translates English transcripts into French sentence by sentence. The worker checks spoken numbers and translates short forms of address separately when the model omits them. Other language pairs use SeamlessM4T v2 Large to translate each speaker’s audio. NLLB translates the verified transcript for words recovered from the original mix, unsupported speech inputs, conflicting voices in overlaps and missing numbers. OmniVoice speaks each translation using that speaker’s reference. The worker checks the generated words and compares sufficiently long multi-speaker takes with the original voice references before mixing. [Helsinki-NLP OPUS-MT](https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-tc-big-en-fr) · [SeamlessM4T v2](https://huggingface.co/facebook/seamless-m4t-v2-large) · [NLLB](https://huggingface.co/facebook/nllb-200-distilled-600M) · [OmniVoice](https://huggingface.co/k2-fsa/OmniVoice)
- With lip sync enabled, YuNet detects faces and SFace matches identities across shots. When a speaker’s identity is ambiguous, Light-ASD compares the original soundtrack with each visible face before choosing whose lips to animate. EDTalk predicts articulation from each translated voice. Facial landmarks move the original lip texture at the source resolution. The model supplies the shape of the mouth interior. When the same speaker appears at a similar angle with similar tooth exposure, the renderer recovers fine surface detail from those source frames. During a pause or after a translated line ends, the renderer aligns a resting view of the same speaker’s lips to the current head position. It uses frames from similar angles and can close the lips on a reference image when the speaker’s mouth is open throughout a shot. [OpenCV Zoo](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo) · [Light-ASD](https://github.com/Junhua-Liao/Light-ASD) · [EDTalk](https://github.com/tanshuai0219/EDTalk) · [EDTalk ONNX model](https://huggingface.co/facefusion/models-3.3.0)

For each speaker, the worker can supplement the current voice with complete phrases from up to two other original recordings. It considers up to 32 catalogued videos that share performer credits, checks clean solo speech against the current speaker and compares it with other speakers in both videos. It uses the current voice alone when it cannot verify a match. The local `dubbing-voices` cache stores short audio samples, transcripts and voice embeddings by source revision, with a 64 MiB payload budget.

Audio-only output copies compatible H.264 video packets with their original timing, orientation and colors. Other formats and damaged recordings use a compatibility encode. With lip sync enabled, the renderer fits mouth width and thickness to the visible speaker and retains the surrounding picture. The MP4 index precedes the picture data for seeking.

If the worker cannot verify the words or the cloned voice after retries, it keeps the original soundtrack and picture for that passage. Overlapping speakers stay together for the affected interval. The player and plugin settings show its timestamps and the reason. A video with no verified translation is reported as a failed job.

Existing dubs remain playable until the replacement is ready. The worker keeps unfinished per-language work for retries. A timing cache of up to 16 MiB per video and language stores compressed isolated translated lines. Enabling lip sync can reuse those lines and the published audio. Disabling it can reuse the published audio directly.

The first run downloads packages and the model weights needed for the job. These include 465 MB for English-to-French OPUS-MT, about 9.2 GB for SeamlessM4T v2 Large when used, and 447 MB for Bandit v2. Subsequent jobs share these files. The worker processes media, text and voice references locally. The pretrained SeamlessM4T, NLLB and OmniVoice models have non-commercial use terms. The Helsinki-NLP OPUS-MT checkpoint uses CC BY 4.0 and the Bandit v2 multilingual weights use CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set `EASYX_DUBBING_DEVICE` to `auto` (default), `cuda`, `mps` or `cpu`. On a shared NVIDIA GPU, the cinematic separator can process channels and independent sequences in smaller batches while keeping the full audio window. It can also run on CPU. Apple Metal can accelerate translation and voice synthesis. For offline provisioning, place the pinned assets under their repository basenames in `EASYX_DUBBING_MODELS` or prepopulate the worker caches. Model revisions are in `runtime.py`. The Light-ASD weights and their SHA-256 are pinned in `active_speaker.py`. Pin the interpreter with `EASYX_DUBBING_PYTHON` when using a custom PyTorch/CUDA build.
