# Local Dubbing

Select output languages in the plugin settings. A separate MP4 per language allows the player to switch both the soundtrack and the synchronized video. English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese are available. A video already entirely in the selected language uses the original. Videos without detected speech are skipped.

## Local processing

- The CDX23 DnR separator extracts a dialogue estimate from the source channels. When the cinematic estimate misses close-microphone speech, a channel-aligned DPDFNet2 estimate removes the remaining dialogue. The soundtrack keeps the background and mixes a separately metered speech track at a target of −18 LUFS. [Cinematic Sound Demixing](https://github.com/ZFTurbo/MVSEP-CDX23-Cinematic-Sound-Demixing) · [Sherpa DPDFNet](https://k2-fsa.github.io/sherpa/onnx/speech-enhancement/dpdfnet.html)
- Sherpa-ONNX clusters turns by speaker. Detected overlaps use local two- or three-voice separation, matched back to speaker embeddings. An entire conversation may include more than three distinct speakers. [Sherpa-ONNX diarization](https://k2-fsa.github.io/sherpa/onnx/speaker-diarization/index.html) · [SepFormer](https://huggingface.co/speechbrain/sepformer-wsj02mix)
- Faster-Whisper transcribes each voice. NLLB translates the text. OmniVoice clones the speaker from complete, cleaned utterances and their transcripts. It generates the requested duration directly and can use up to 1.25 seconds of an available pause to finish a translation. [Faster-Whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper) · [NLLB-200 model card](https://huggingface.co/facebook/nllb-200-distilled-600M) · [OmniVoice model card](https://huggingface.co/k2-fsa/OmniVoice)
- YuNet detects faces and SFace matches identities across shots. EDTalk predicts articulation from each translated voice. Facial landmarks move the original lip texture at the source resolution. The model supplies the shape of the mouth interior. When the same speaker appears at a similar angle with similar tooth exposure, the renderer recovers fine surface detail from those source frames. During a pause or after a translated line ends, the renderer aligns a resting view of the same speaker’s lips to the current head position. It uses frames from similar angles and can close the lips on a reference image when the speaker’s mouth is open throughout a shot. [OpenCV Zoo](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo) · [EDTalk](https://github.com/tanshuai0219/EDTalk) · [EDTalk ONNX model](https://huggingface.co/facefusion/models-3.3.0)

The MP4 keeps the source frame timing, display orientation and color metadata. For even-sized frames with standard rotations, the encoder takes the picture directly from the decoded YUV planes and composites the mouth edits locally. H.264 uses CRF 14 to retain fine texture.

The workers fetch models and packages on first use. Video, audio, text and speaker references are processed locally. The NLLB and OmniVoice pretrained models have non-commercial use terms. Each media revision has its own work directory. Completed language outputs are published by atomic rename. Source audio, speaker references and intermediate tracks are removed once the selected languages finish. A failed or interrupted render keeps its intermediate files so it can resume.

Set `EASYX_DUBBING_DEVICE` to `auto` (default), `cuda`, `mps` or `cpu`. The cinematic separator uses CUDA or CPU. Speech transcription uses CUDA or CPU. Apple Metal can accelerate translation and voice synthesis. For offline provisioning, place the pinned assets under their repository basenames in `EASYX_DUBBING_MODELS` or prepopulate the worker caches. Model revisions are in `runtime.py`. Pin the interpreter with `EASYX_DUBBING_PYTHON` when using a custom PyTorch/CUDA build.
